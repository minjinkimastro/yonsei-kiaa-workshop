About the workshop

Galaxy evolution from nearby systems to the high-redshift Universe

The Yonsei-KIAA Workshop on Galaxy Evolution is designed as an intensive forum for new results, cross-institution collaboration, and discussion of emerging facilities and surveys.

Topics span high-z galaxies, little red dots, AGNs, nearby galaxies, CSST, 7DS, and SPHEREx, with a combination of invited presentations, contributed talks, hands-on activity, and discussion sessions.

Scientific themes

01

High-z Galaxies

Formation, morphology, structure, and evolution in the early Universe.

02

Little Red Dots

Emerging constraints on compact red sources and their AGN connection.

03

AGNs

Black-hole growth, variability, obscuration, hosts, and feedback.

04

Nearby Galaxies

Resolved studies that connect local physics to cosmic evolution.

05

CSST & 7DS

Survey strategies, datasets, and new opportunities for galaxy science.

06

SPHEREx

Infrared spectrophotometric mapping of galaxies, dust, and AGNs.

Transportation

Getting to Baekyang Nuri

01

Subway

Take Seoul Subway Line 2 to Sinchon Station. Use Exit 2 or 3, walk toward Yonsei University, enter through the Main Gate, and continue along Baekyang-ro to Baekyang Nuri.

02

Bus

Many city buses stop at Yonsei University or the Yonsei University Main Gate. Useful routes include blue buses 163, 171, 172 and green buses 7017, 7737; other routes also serve the campus.

03

From Incheon Airport

Airport Bus 6011 serves the Yonsei University area. A taxi is also convenient; show the destination as “연세대학교 백양누리 IBK홀”.

04

Taxi / Navigation

For taxi or navigation apps, search for “Baekyang Nuri, Yonsei University” or “연세대학교 백양누리”. The workshop room is on B1.
