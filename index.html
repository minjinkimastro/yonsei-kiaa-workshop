About the workshop

Galaxy evolution from nearby systems to the high-redshift Universe

The Yonsei-KIAA Workshop on Galaxy Evolution is designed as an intensive forum for new results, cross-institution collaboration, and discussion of emerging facilities and surveys.

Topics span high-z galaxies, little red dots, AGNs, nearby galaxies, CSST, 7DS, and SPHEREx, with a combination of invited presentations, contributed talks, hands-on activity, and discussion sessions.

Scientific themes

01

High-z Galaxies

Formation, morphology, structure, and evolution in the early Universe.

02

Little Red Dots

Emerging constraints on compact red sources and their AGN connection.

03

AGNs

Black-hole growth, variability, obscuration, hosts, and feedback.

04

Nearby Galaxies

Resolved studies that connect local physics to cosmic evolution.

05

CSST & 7DS

Survey strategies, datasets, and new opportunities for galaxy science.

06

SPHEREx

Infrared spectrophotometric mapping of galaxies, dust, and AGNs.

Transportation

Getting to Baekyang Nuri

01

Subway

Take Seoul Subway Line 2 to Sinchon Station. Use Exit 2 or 3, walk toward Yonsei University, enter through the Main Gate, and continue along Baekyang-ro to Baekyang Nuri.

02

Bus

Many city buses stop at Yonsei University or the Yonsei University Main Gate. Useful routes include blue buses 163, 171, 172 and green buses 7017, 7737; other routes also serve the campus.

03

From Incheon Airport

Airport Bus 6011 serves the Yonsei University area. A taxi may be convenient but expensive (70-100 USD); show the destination as “연세대학교 백양누리 IBK홀”.

04

Taxi / Navigation

For taxi or navigation apps, search for “Baekyang Nuri, Yonsei University” or “연세대학교 백양누리”. The workshop room is on B1.

Explore Seoul

Recommended places to visit

If you have some free time before or after the workshop, Seoul offers an easy mix of contemporary culture, royal palaces, traditional neighborhoods, museums, shopping, and K-culture landmarks. The following places are especially convenient and rewarding for international visitors.

01

Hongdae · 홍대

A lively university district close to Yonsei, known for cafés, restaurants, shopping, street performances, music, and nightlife. An easy evening trip from Sinchon.

View on Google Maps →

02

National Museum of Korea · 국립중앙박물관

Korea’s flagship museum for Korean history and art in Yongsan. A particularly good choice for visitors who want an introduction to Korean cultural heritage.

View on Google Maps →

03

Gyeongbokgung Palace · 경복궁

The largest of Seoul’s Five Grand Palaces and one of the city’s essential historic sights. It pairs naturally with Gwanghwamun, Bukchon, and Insadong.

View on Google Maps →

04

Changdeokgung Palace · 창덕궁

A beautifully preserved Joseon royal palace, especially famous for its architecture and the landscaped Secret Garden (Huwon). A highlight for visitors interested in historic Seoul.

View on Google Maps →

05

Deoksugung Palace · 덕수궁

A compact royal palace in central Seoul known for its mix of traditional Korean and Western-style buildings. Its stone-wall road is also pleasant for a short walk.

View on Google Maps →

06

Changgyeonggung Palace · 창경궁

A quieter royal palace next to Changdeokgung, with spacious grounds and a more relaxed atmosphere. It can be combined with Changdeokgung and Jongmyo.

View on Google Maps →

07

Bukchon Hanok Village · 북촌한옥마을

A historic residential neighborhood of traditional hanok houses between the royal palaces. Please keep voices low and respect residents while exploring its narrow lanes.

View on Google Maps →

08

Insadong · 인사동

A traditional arts and culture district with galleries, crafts, ceramics, souvenirs, tea houses, and Ssamzigil. It pairs naturally with Bukchon and the palaces.

View on Google Maps →

09

Jongmyo Shrine · 종묘

A UNESCO-listed royal ancestral shrine of the Joseon Dynasty, noted for its restrained architecture, ritual traditions, and tranquil grounds.

View on Google Maps →

10

Myeongdong · 명동

One of Seoul’s best-known shopping districts, with Korean cosmetics, fashion, department stores, cafés, restaurants, and street food. It is also convenient for visiting Namsan and N Seoul Tower.

View on Google Maps →

11

Cheonggyecheon Stream · 청계천

A landscaped urban stream running through central Seoul, ideal for a relaxed walk between sightseeing stops. The western section near Cheonggye Plaza is especially easy to combine with Gwanghwamun, Insadong, and Myeongdong.

View on Google Maps →

12

KPop Demon Hunters · 케데헌

Fans can explore Seoul landmarks associated with KPop Demon Hunters, including N Seoul Tower and Lotte World Tower. These make a fun K-culture addition to a Seoul visit.
